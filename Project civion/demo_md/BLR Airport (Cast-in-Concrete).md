## 1.3 SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

## A. Performance Requirements

1. Formwork Design: Design formwork, shoring and bracing to sustain construction and wind loads and maintain concrete lines and levels within concrete tolerances specified herein. Formwork types are labeled according to concrete surface finishes. Provide formwork types and construction to obtain the class of surface indicated and as required by referenced standards. Design forms to permit easy removal. Make the forms sufficiently rigid and strong enough to withstand, without leakage, the high hydraulic pressures which result from rapid filling and heavy high-frequency vibration. Deflection of each formwork component shall be limited to 1/360 of the component span due to hydraulic pressure of concrete.

## B. Sustainable Design [and LEED] Performance Criteria

1. Credit MR 5, Regional Materials: The manufacturing locations and origin of raw materials for the cat-in-place concrete systems shall be identified and documented if within if sourced within a straight-line 800 kilometers total travel distance to the project site.
2. Credit MR 4, Recycled Content: The post-industrial and/or post-consumer recycled content (by weight) of the major cast-in-place concrete components shall be identified and documented.
- a. Recycled Content: Metal products shall strive to achieve a recycled content of 50% to 100%.
- b. Recycled Content: Concrete products shall strive to achieve a fly ash content of above 30%, but shall not exceed the requirements of IS 1489 (Part 1).

## 1.6 DELIVERY, STORAGE AND HANDLING

- A. Packing, Shipping, Handling, and Unloading: Use materials from the same source from start to finish of job unless changes have been accepted by the ER. Deliver and store materials in manufacturer's original packaging labeled to show name, brand, type, and grade. Store, materials in protected dry location off ground in accordance with manufacturer's instructions. stockpile and use aggregate in a manner which minimizes moisture content variation. Concrete producer and suppliers shall load, transport, handle, store and batch concrete materials in a manner that will ensure the materials are not contaminated, unclean, segregated or affected in a way that will damage the final product.
1. Bagged Cement: Store on raised platforms in a dry area. Use bagged cement on the basis of first received, first used. Do not store bagged cement over 6 months.
2. Reinforcement: Deliver reinforcement to the job site bundled, tagged and marked. Use metal tags indicating bar size, lengths and other information corresponding to markings shown on shop drawings. Store reinforcement at the job-site in a manner to prevent damage and accumulation of dirt and excessive rust.

## 1.7 PROJECT/SITE CONDITIONS

- A. Hot or Cold Weather Concreting: Comply as follows:
1. During hot or cold weather the concreting should be done as per the procedures set out in IS 7861 Part I or IS 7861 Part II or as directed by the ER.
2. Review with the ER the methods of protection and/or mix design modifications to be used for hot weather concreting. This review shall occur prior to the mean daily temperature exceeding (27 deg. C.)
3. When the temperature is above (32 deg. C.), reduce the mixing/delivery time from 11/2 hrs. to 1-1/4 hrs. Cool forms to a maximum of (27 deg. C.) Cool concrete to maximum of (21 deg. C.) leaving the mixer.
4. Do not place concrete at a temperature greater than (35 deg. C.)
5. When ambient placement temperatures are more than (27 deg. C.), provide waterreducing, set-retarding admixture in accordance manufacturer's recommendations
- B. Concreting In Aggressive Soils and Water:
1. Guidelines laid down in 13.4 of IS 456 shall be followed together with the instruction of the ER.

- D. Concealed Formed Surfaces: Remove fins, and patch tie holes.
- E. Exposed Formed Surfaces, Unpainted: Remove fins, patch tie holes, and stone the joint marks and out-of-plane surfaces to within 3 mm of flush. Rub with carborundum stone using only enough water to develop cement past from concrete mortar and to produce uniformly dense and smooth concrete.
- F. Exposed Formed Surfaces, Painted: Remove fins, patch tie holes, and stone the joint marks and out-of-plane surfaces to within 1.5 mm of flush. Rub with carborundum stone using only enough water to develop a cement paste from the concrete mortar and to produce uniformly dense and smooth concrete.
- G. Exposed Formed Surfaces, Smooth As-Cast Finish: Maintain concrete free from laitance caused by spillage, leaking forms or other contaminants. Do not allow laitance to penetrate, stain or harden on finished surfaces. Do not attempt surface patching or cleaning, if required, unless acceptable to Architect and ER. Patch tie holes to match the surrounding surfaces and strike of flush.
